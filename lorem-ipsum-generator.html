How to Use

  1. Choose a Unit — paragraphs for full blocks of text, sentences for individual lines, or words for short snippets.
  2. Set the Amount — how many paragraphs, sentences, or words you need.
  3. Toggle "Start with Lorem ipsum…" to begin with the classic opening phrase, or generate fully randomized text.
  4. Enable Wrap in <p> tags if you need HTML-ready output for direct use in code.
  5. Click Generate, then Copy to paste the text into your project.

About This Tool

Lorem Ipsum is standard placeholder text used by designers, developers, and publishers since the 1500s. It originates from a scrambled passage of "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" by Cicero. Its advantage over real text is that it doesn't distract reviewers from the visual layout — the eye focuses on spacing, typography, and composition instead of reading the content. This generator produces Lorem Ipsum text entirely in your browser using a randomized word pool drawn from the classic source. No data is sent to any server. Choose paragraphs for full content areas, sentences for captions and labels, or words for button copy and heading tests.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy text derived from a 1st-century BC Latin text by Cicero. It has been used as placeholder text in typesetting and design since the 1960s when Letraset sheets popularized it. It is now the industry standard for mockups and prototypes because it approximates the visual weight of natural language without carrying meaningful content that could distract reviewers.

Is Lorem Ipsum text always the same?

The classic opening phrase — "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit" — is standardized and widely recognized. However, subsequent paragraphs are typically randomized from a pool of Latin-derived words, so each generated block is unique. This generator randomizes all text except for the optional classic opener.

Can I use the <p> tag output directly in HTML?

Yes. Enable Wrap in <p> tags and the output will be formatted as <p>...</p> blocks, ready to paste into your HTML template. Each paragraph gets its own tag, making it immediately usable in any web project without further editing.
